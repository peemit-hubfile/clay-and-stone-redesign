FAQ
## Frequently Asked Questions
##### 1. What is a prefab home?
A
modular prefab home
  -> (https://youtu.be/Uo16eHa15rk?si=PHymmVjCB_HOAjRE)
is a construction method where the main components are manufactured in a factory under controlled conditions, then assembled on-site. At Clay & Stone, our modular prefab house builds use a steel frame structure as the core, which is strong, durable, and well-suited to factory production. This means every home comes out precise and consistent, not dependent on site conditions or the weather. Once production is complete, components are transported and installed on-site in around 15 days before the finishing and handover stages begin.
‍
##### 2. How is a prefab home different from conventional construction?
The primary differences lie in the construction methodology and quality control. Traditional on-site construction happens sequentially outdoors, making it highly vulnerable to weather delays and inconsistent labor quality. In contrast, a Clay & Stone modern modular house is precision-built inside a factory by a dedicated team of professionals monitoring every stage. This minimizes structural errors, guarantees uniform quality, and ensures a highly predictable, accurate delivery timeline that conventional builds simply cannot match.
‍
##### 3. How strong is a Clay & Stone prefab home?
When considering the
strength of prefab houses
  -> (https://youtu.be/gJMY5oSiwbQ?si=fTxp-1qL5mLzb3mW)
, structural integrity is often a major concern for homebuyers. At Clay & Stone, engineering strength is our top priority. Every modular house we build features a high-grade, structurally engineered steel frame paired with carefully selected wall materials designed for long-term performance. This engineering approach allows our homes to withstand high wind loads and seismic vibrations while delivering long-term durability, safety, and permanence comparable to a traditional brick-and-mortar house.
‍
##### 4. What modular house designs does Clay & Stone offer?
Clay & Stone currently offers two signature modular house collections: the
Unwind Series
  -> (https://www.clayandstone.co.th/en/products/unwind-series)
and the Alive Series, each designed to support different lifestyles and ways of living. Homeowners can choose from a range of layouts, including Standard and Loft configurations, with both 1-bedroom and 2-bedroom options available across selected models.
Every modern modular house design is thoughtfully developed for Thailand’s climate, incorporating features such as extended roof overhangs, functional verandas, and design details that elevate the living experience beyond what is typically found in the prefab market.
‍
##### 5. What does after-sales service and warranty look like?
You're covered. After-sales care is something we take seriously at Clay & Stone, and we handle it directly, not passed off to outside contractors. Every customer who builds a modular house in Thailand can rely on us being reachable long after handover. If something comes up after you've moved in, our technical team will come out, inspect, and sort it out.
