## Clay & Stone
### is a prefabricated home builder
Founded by a professional team of architects, engineers, and construction specialists, Clay & Stone brings design and build expertise together under one roof. We focus on every detail — from functional planning and aesthetics to long-term durability — to create homes that are truly comfortable, lasting, and designed for real living.
### What We Do
## 01
#### Homes designed using a standardized modular system.
## 02
#### Home modules manufactured in a factory with consistent quality standards.
## 03
#### Modules transported to the site in one complete delivery.
## 04
#### On-site assembly and final installation.
