# Returning Nature to Living
# A modular home designed to connect you with nature.
The Unwind Series by
Clay & Stone
  -> (https://www.clayandstone.co.th/en)
is a collection of modular homes for sale built around one simple idea: living well means living close to nature. Designed using a standardized modular system, each home allows for faster construction, predictable costs, and the flexibility to adapt as life evolves. Whether you're looking for a weekend retreat, a second home on your land, or a resort unit, the Unwind Series offers a modular house design that balances warmth, simplicity, and lasting quality.

## A Modular Home You Can Customize
The Unwind Series is far from a one-size-fits-all solution. Built on a flexible modular system, it allows you to freely combine individual modules to match your exact lifestyle. Whether you want to add an extra bedroom or expand your outdoor living terrace, the design can adapt seamlessly without the need for a full reconstruction. Plus, every module is engineered for easy relocation, allowing you to disassemble and reinstall your home in a brand-new location whenever you choose.

## Materials
Every single material in the Unwind Series is handpicked for long-term durability and comfort, not just initial aesthetic appeal. Each component is engineered to work together as a synchronized system, perfectly mirroring our
modular construction process
  -> (https://www.clayandstone.co.th/en/process)
, where every step is designed to connect with absolute precision.
##### 01/ Structural System
[IMG alt=""] https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693f3e67a692e733b206630e/694acd6b802a0ed904111e67_unwind-img-06.webp
Structural steel over 2.3 mm thick, certified to TIS standards and up to 1.4x thicker than typical specifications, ensuring long-term strength. Protected with high-performance anti-corrosion coatings from BEGER, and designed and supervised by licensed architects and engineers.
##### 02/ Flooring System
[IMG alt=""] https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693f3e67a692e733b206630e/694acd6b82786849ab80c623_unwind-img-05.webp
Steel floor joists are spaced at 40 cm for even load distribution and stability. Finished with 20 mm SCG Smart Board and 5 mm SCG SPC Click Lock flooring, creating a solid and firm surface that feels stable and comfortable underfoot.
##### 03/ Wall Materials
[IMG alt=""] https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693f3e67a692e733b206630e/694acd6b56f80fe66b21adca_unwind-img-04.webp

### Frequently Asked Questions
##### 1. Why choose the Unwind Series modular home?
The Unwind Series is envisioned and crafted under the concept of true relaxation. It features an open, airy modular house design that seamlessly connects your indoor living space with the natural beauty outside. It is perfect as a vacation home, a boutique resort villa, or a private sanctuary in your backyard garden.
##### 2. What are the modular house prices, and what types are available?
For Clay & Stone's Unwind Series, we offer a diverse range of sizes engineered to match your specific plot and budget requirements. Our transparent, fixed modular house prices are detailed below (all prices include 7% VAT):
‍
Standard Type:
830,000 THB (Usable Area: 43.5 sqm)

1,190,000 THB (Usable Area: 47 sqm)

Investing in a modular system not only guarantees premium-grade materials, but it also eliminates unexpected budget overruns and saves a massive amount of construction time compared to conventional brick-and-mortar builds.
##### 3. Which type works best for a small family?
We highly recommend our most popular model:
the Unwind 1-Bed, 2-Bath
  -> (https://youtu.be/G8_2lxRIVV4?si=7aPKjFsS-APmXwD9)
. The layout is beautifully proportioned and zoned for daily life. It features a master bedroom with an en-suite bathroom, a guest restroom, a central living area for family bonding, and a dedicated kitchen space. It perfectly suits small families seeking both daily convenience and personal privacy.
##### 4. Can modular homes be installed on any plot of land? Can they be relocated in the future?
Yes! Clay & Stone modular homes are designed to be transported and installed almost anywhere in Thailand as long as crane or truck access is available. Our engineering team assesses every site before installation to ensure a smooth and safe process. If you ever need to move the home to a new plot, the
modular system
  -> (https://youtu.be/0MDRduX63pY?si=TyG9_q_u4MesLR_j)
allows full disassembly and reinstallation, no demolition required. That flexibility is one of the core advantages of the Unwind Series.
##### 5. How long does the process take from order to move-in?
The primary benefits of our prefabricated homes are speed, structural consistency, and strict quality control. The Clay & Stone delivery process is broken down into 4 clear phases:
‍
Step 1: Tailored Customization:
Our architectural team works closely with you to adjust and finalize your layout, ensuring the final layout supports your functional needs perfectly before purchase.
‍
Step 2: Agreement & Off-Site Production:
Once the contract is signed, precision manufacturing begins inside our factory right away (taking roughly 60 - 90 days depending on the current production queue). Factory building ensures flawless craftsmanship unaffected by unpredictable weather conditions, keeping our timelines perfectly on track.
Step 3: Transporting the Modules:
When factory production reaches approximately 70% completion, the structural modules are carefully transported to your land. Because the heavy lifting is handled off-site, there is no need for messy, massive construction works on your property, drastically reducing dust, noise, and environmental disturbance to your neighbors.
Step 4: On-Site Assembly & Handover:
Our specialized crew handles the final installation, structural sealing, and utility connections on-site. Within just 15 days, your turn-key home is completely ready for move-in.
‍
With this system, you will never have to worry about the stressful delays, unreliable contractors, or budget uncertainties of traditional construction.
