Are you thinking of building a new house? This era tells us that there are a lot of interesting options, especially the trend of building finished houses, without having to wait for years like traditional brick masonry houses. One of the most popular questions that many people wonder a lot when searching for information is “What is the difference between a modular house and a knockdown house?” The exterior also looks similar, it's the same prefabricated house. So what's the difference? Which one to choose will be the most profitable and suitable for us?
In this article, Clay & Stone will take everyone on an easy to understand look at how modular homes and knockdown homes differ, who each is suitable for, and what to look out for before making a decision.
##### 4 Resort Style Knockdown House Ideas From The Unwind Series
Unwind Series
  -> (https://www.clayandstone.co.th/th/products/unwind-series)
Designed so that the house is not just “built quickly”, but a space that you want to return to... On a daily basis, all 4 types of knockdown houses can be practically adapted as a private residence, garden house or even made into a small resort.
###### 1) Standard Type: Simple but perfect for a private holiday
[IMG alt=""] https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a09582bef83c88724230a19/6a099477cef91fd425327600_1.webp
For those looking for a small knockdown home that feels peaceful and private, the Standard Type is the perfect answer, with an accentuated design. Minimalism cuts out the unnecessary, leaving only practical functions. Compact space, ideal for a holiday home or a private retreat corner where you can fully return to “yourself”.
###### 2) Loft Type: Increase the living dimension with high frost design, airy, comfortable
[IMG alt=""] https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a09582bef83c88724230a19/6a099488b1989f88efdfe9d6_2.webp
Loft Type is ideal for people who like the feeling of “breathing space”. Thanks to the high ceiling design, it allows the interior to look more airy, open and comfortable. Natural light has good access, allowing the interior atmosphere to change according to the time of day. Ideal for a holiday home or a house where Want to create a special feeling like staying at a resort every time you return.
###### 3) 1 Bed 2 Bath: clearly proportioned, accommodates resort use
[IMG alt=""] https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a09582bef83c88724230a19/6a099497de3395691da2db36_3.webp
For those looking to elevate their living experience or plan to upgrade to a knockdown home, this Type Resort is designed with 1 bedroom and 2 bathrooms, allowing for a more convenient and proportionate use, perfect for accommodating guests or creating a resort-level feel. Premium that offers both privacy and comfort in every use
###### 4) 2 Bed 2 Bath: Small family home in a cozy atmosphere
[IMG alt=""] https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a09582bef83c88724230a19/6a0994a3f1a88a8406e68737_4.webp
If you want to have a home where you can spend time with your family, 2 Bed 2 Bath is the perfect choice. With the space allocated to two bedrooms, it is ideal for living together. Whether it is a holiday retreat, a second home or a resort, this house will become a place of good memories that everyone can always spend together.

With a clear price structure from the start, it allows you to plan your budget with confidence. ##### Choosing a resort knockdown house is worth more than you think.
For investors or business owners looking to start a place to stay, Knockdown House is the right choice for these reasons.
Sensitive Payback:
The construction period is very fast. Compared to stucco masonry construction, you can be open to guests and start making money. several times faster
100% cost control:
No more problems with “late statements”. Don't worry about uncontrollable labor prices or building material prices. THAT FLUCTUATES ALL THE TIME
High Flexibility:
This is the best advantage if you want to modify your business plan or change your location in the future. Resort Knockdown can be immediately disassembled and moved to a new piece of land, without losing the value of an empty house like a typical permanent building.
