## What Factors Affect the Price of a Modular House?
Even two homes of similar size can vary in price for several reasons:
### 1. Floor Area
Floor area is the single biggest factor affecting price - the larger the space, the more materials and construction work required.
That said, choosing a home larger than you actually need can push your budget up unnecessarily. It's always worth starting by analyzing your real usage needs first - how many rooms you need, and how many people will be living there.
### 2. Interior Functions
Homes with the same floor area can vary in price depending on the number of bathrooms, bedrooms, or special-use spaces.
For example,
a home with 2 bathrooms, a full kitchen, or a dedicated laundry area will naturally cost more than one focused on the basics.
### 3. Materials and Finishes
The materials you choose affect appearance, lifespan, and budget all at once. Beyond aesthetics, it's worth considering suitability for Thailand's climate, resistance to humidity and sunlight, and ease of long-term maintenance.
### 4. Structural System
The structural system is at the heart of every modular home, since it directly affects strength, safety, and long-term lifespan.
Every brand may have different material standards. For example, Clay & Stone uses TIS-standard steel structures that are thicker than typical specs, along with a premium anti-rust coating system to help protect the structure from Thailand's hot, humid climate. So beyond comparing prices, it's worth considering structural quality, materials, and manufacturing standards together, since these directly affect durability and long-term maintenance costs.
### 5. Transportation and Installation
Modular houses are manufactured at the factory and installed on site, so distance and site conditions can add extra transportation and installation costs in certain cases - especially for hard-to-access locations or sites requiring special equipment for delivery and setup.
## Additional Costs to Plan For
Beyond the cost of the house itself, there are other expenses worth planning for from the start to avoid budget overruns later.
### Site Preparation
If your land hasn't been leveled or filled, there may be additional costs for preparing the site before installation.
### Foundation Work
Even though modular homes are lighter than conventional construction, they still need a foundation suited to the soil conditions of each site.
Many builders today are shifting to precast foundation systems to reduce construction time and improve quality control. At Clay & Stone, we use a factory-made precast foundation system that reduces on-site concrete pouring, keeps the construction site tidier, ensures better quality control, and allows for faster installation than traditional foundation methods.
### Electrical and Plumbing Systems
This includes meter installation, water supply and drainage pipes, septic tanks, and utility connections - all of which should be planned from the start to avoid unexpected costs later.
For many homeowners, coordinating multiple contractors can be time-consuming and detail-heavy, which is why many prefer a provider who can manage the building systems alongside the house construction itself, keeping the process smooth and easier to control for quality.
### Landscaping and Outdoor Areas
This includes walkways, gardens, fencing, or outdoor seating areas - a budget item many people forget to account for early on. In reality, outdoor space affects both the atmosphere and functionality of a home just as much as the house itself. Planning your landscaping alongside your home design from the start creates a more cohesive project, both aesthetically and functionally, while keeping the budget under control.
### Furniture and Appliances
Even though the house itself is move-in ready, actually settling in still requires a budget for interior decor and appliances based on your needs.
For those wanting extra convenience, many providers today offer interior design and built-in furniture services alongside the home build, which helps keep the interior design cohesive with the overall architecture and makes the space more efficient to use. Clay & Stone has a team that can handle and coordinate this for you as well.

## How Much Extra Budget Should You Set Aside?
As a general rule, set aside an additional 20-30% of the cost of the house itself for site preparation, foundation work, utility systems, and related outdoor work.
Planning your budget thoroughly from the start reduces the risk of unexpected costs and helps your project move forward smoothly.
## The Right House Is the One That Fits Your Needs
Choosing a modular house isn't about picking the biggest or most feature-packed option - it should start with understanding your lifestyle, budget, and real needs. Whether it's a compact getaway home, a second home, or a family residence, choosing the right size from the start ensures you get space that's genuinely worth the investment and suited for long-term living.
