# Competitive Benchmarking: Plan

**Stage 1 · Discovery** · 20 Nov 2026 · Status: **plan only. Research not yet executed.**

> Per the stage gate, Stage 1 delivers the *plan* for benchmarking. The comparative findings will be added to this file in Stage 2, after the shortlist below is approved. The brands listed are candidates chosen for relevance. No claims about their sites are made here until they have been reviewed.

## 1. Objective

Find proven patterns for **(a)** presenting a small catalogue of homes as desirable products, **(b)** making price, specs and inclusions easy to understand, **(c)** building trust in factory-built construction, and **(d)** turning interest into a consultation. These patterns then get adapted to Clay & Stone's own identity: warm tropical modernism, deep eaves, verandas, Thai climate. **Not copied.**

## 3. Evaluation framework

Each site is reviewed on desktop and mobile, in the visitor's role, using the same 10 dimensions from the brief. Each dimension is scored 1–5 with screenshot evidence:

| Dimension | What we look for |
|---|---|
| Brand positioning | What is said in the first viewport; clarity of who it's for |
| Homepage experience | Hierarchy, hero, path to products, proof |
| Product discovery | Catalogue structure, filters, comparison, price visibility |
| Product detail page | Plans, dimensions, inclusions, gallery, specs, CTA placement |
| Navigation | Depth, labels, mobile menu, language switching |
| Storytelling | Process, materials, founders, projects |
| Photography | Art direction, crops, interior/exterior balance, human presence |
| Interaction design | Motion restraint, configurators, micro-interactions |
| Conversion strategy | CTA types, forms vs chat, friction, reassurance near CTAs |
| Mobile experience | Thumb reach, performance feel, sticky actions |

Plus three Clay & Stone-specific lenses:
- **Price and inclusion transparency**
- **Trust in factory-built quality**
- **Bilingual handling** (where applicable)

## 4. Method and rules

- Public pages only. No sign-ups and no form submissions.
- Capture reference screenshots for internal analysis only. They will not be published in the prototype.
- Record **patterns**, not layouts. Each finding becomes: *pattern → why it works → how it maps to Clay & Stone → what we will NOT copy*.
- Note the date of review, since sites change.
